    Technical Breakdown / Stack

    • Post-Production: Davinci Resolve

    Lessons Learned

    Being the first part of my project. I chose to work with Davinci Resolve. I chose to do so in order to broaden my skill set and knowledge in post-production software. I have learned how to effectively color grade, vignette, audio mix and manage the timeline using Davinci's UI.

    One important lesson I have learned is to keep on-screen text within the title-safe areas, as it may get cut off on different screens or playback platforms, as well as to be more careful with text colors and contrast.

    Promotional Video - Jef Neve Scores

    Visual Showcase

    *Final render, 1080p at 25fps, aspect ratio 16:9.*

    Objective

    "Create a promotional video for 3 piano books 'Start 2 Play', composed by Belgian pianist and composer Jef Neve. The result should be recruiting."

    Technical Breakdown / Stack

    • Post-Production: Final Cut Pro

    Lessons Learned

    I continued expanding my horizons by switching to Final Cut Pro for my second project. My aim was to create dynamic and rhythmic musical cutting between the multicamera shots, the audio and the voiceover.

    I synchronized the camera angles of the composition in a multicamera source sequence, audio mixed the voiceover and background music, color graded and matched graphics with the voiceover.

    This project has taught me to better manage multicamera sequences and pay close attention to the relevancy and timing of cuts in relation to the music and voiceover.

    Technical Breakdown / Stack

    • Post-Production: Adobe Premiere Pro

    Lessons Learned

    For my final video, I decided to return to Adobe Premiere Pro, as I was already familiar with this software. My aim for this project was to create a mysterious noir film scene that effectively conveyed tension and atmosphere through careful score-to-shot pacing and color grading.

    I picked my score carefully, as well as chose to use a LUT mask: the "Fuji REALA 500D Kodak 2393". This LUT properly conveys the ambience I wanted to achieve for my scene color-wise as well as adds the necessary film grain to create a noir aesthetic.

    This project has taught me the importance of pacing and ambience in storytelling through video. I've felt firsthand how much of a difference the right music and color grading can make in setting the tone and mood of a scene.
