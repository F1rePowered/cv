    Short Summary

    For this project, I created two unique logo reveal animations with the use of: track mattes, mask animations, and trim paths with the objective of achieving smooth revealing effects.

    Technical Breakdown / Stack

    • Software: Adobe After Effects

    Methodology

    Logomotion A was made by individually animating logo components via keyframing mask paths, scale, opacity & position for separate mask sections.

    For Logomotion B, I used a vector extension logo as a track matte for a shape layer with trim paths. The masked elements were keyframed to achieve a progressive reveal.
